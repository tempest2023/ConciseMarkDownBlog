## Design Context

### Users
Visitors to Tempest's public blog, including recruiters, research peers, and potential collaborators. The personal agent helps them ask focused questions about public work, research, and experience without leaving the site.

### Brand Personality
Calm, credible, and editorial. The agent should feel like a natural extension of the existing personal blog rather than a separate AI product.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the blog's warm paper palette, serif-and-humanist typography, restrained borders, and quiet visual hierarchy in light and dark themes. Use familiar chat conventions for message presentation and composition, but do not imitate ChatGPT wholesale or add unrelated controls such as voice input.

### Design Principles
- Keep the onboarding state inviting, then give the conversation the full available canvas after the first message.
- Make replies, history, current model, and conversation controls immediately legible.
- Prefer a compact, text-first composer with one clear Send action.
- Use brief, purposeful state transitions and respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Store chat history locally in the visitor's browser and describe that privacy boundary accurately.
